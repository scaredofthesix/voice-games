# Voice Games - Product Roadmap

A living, high-level view of where the product is going. Detailed work lives in GitHub
Issues and the Sprint board; this file gives the customer and team the big picture.

## Now - MVP v1 (Sprint 1, 15-21 Jun 2026)
- US-01 Pick a game from the hub.
- US-02 Play Voice Racer with voice steering.
- US-04 Play Voice Bubble Popper by speaking words.
- US-07 See score and lives during play.
- US-08 Reliable speech recognition (lenient matching) - issue #35.
- Bubble speed tuned for younger players - issue #34.

## Next
- US-09 / US-15 Bilingual (EN/RU) word support: Russian translations and clickable
  translation overlay - issues #36, #37.
- Per-word study scorecard improvements after each round.
- Public deployment reachable outside the Innopolis network.

## Later
- More word categories and difficulty modes.
- Progress saving across sessions.
- Accessibility pass (keyboard fallback, captions for audio cues).

_Priorities follow the customer-approved MoSCoW scope from the Week 2 review and may shift
between sprints based on customer feedback._
